# Suite implementation

This document describes the implementation on `main`. [gaps.md](gaps.md) remains the historical assessment of the original MVP preserved on `main-mvp`; its source references describe that baseline, not the current suite. The new suite uses fresh databases and volumes and provides no MVP data import.

## Design system

The web app adopts the Lixionary Design System v1.1.0 foundations from Jira's `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css` and the accessible controls and status colors from IAM's `frontend/src/styles/index.css`. `apps/web/src/app/tokens.css` holds the local foundation; `globals.css` adds reporting-specific layouts, responsive styles and a dark palette. Changes to these local copies are reviewed independently of the source applications.

Cormorant Garamond headings, Inter body text and JetBrains Mono secrets are bundled using `next/font/local`. The Latin WOFF2 files come from IAM's Fontsource 5.3.0 packages; their SIL Open Font License files are included under `apps/web/src/app/fonts`. Browser font requests stay on the application origin. The shared shell, forms, status badges and tables use the same foundation across reporter and staff screens, with visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion and forced-color overrides.

## Product decisions

One company per deployment; registered employee email OTP admission before accountless, anonymous reports; English and Indonesian. Employee eligibility records are administered separately from staff workspace accounts. Optional case contacts are hidden from all application staff. OTP confirms destination control, not legal identity. Reporter recovery uses a separately saved recovery secret, never an email or phone lookup. Staff use local passwords and mandatory authenticator MFA. System administrators manage the company but cannot read case narratives or evidence. Managers review cases in their current team; analysts need a current team membership and explicit assignment. Recusal removes access. Leadership or review-team allegations go to an independent restricted team with no overlapping memberships. A different manager must approve closure.

The assistant is optional and off by default. Both company enablement and server-recorded reporter consent precede processing. Only the reviewed report is published to staff. Evidence originals remain sealed in the private service; staff receive only sanitized, reporter-approved derivatives. Retention is mandatory company configuration in production. Legal holds suspend deletion. Delivery supports SMTP or Zoho CPaaS HTTP email and Twilio Messaging. Operations target a Docker VPS with offsite encrypted restic backups.

## Limits to communicate honestly

- Narrative, circumstances, message timing, and visible evidence may identify someone despite contact isolation. Reporters must review every page; sanitization cannot infer every identity clue.
- Privileged host/database/key operators and delivery providers are outside the application-role anonymity boundary. Separating operational access and protecting hosts/backups remains necessary.
- Losing both saved reporter secrets makes a case unrecoverable. No staff override reveals or recreates access. Reloading an unsaved receipt also loses its memory-only secrets.
- Redaction flattens PDF/Office documents to images, removing selectable text, signatures and interactive content. Originals are preserved sealed while retention or legal hold applies; ordinary staff cannot retrieve them.
- An interrupted submission can be retried in the same page using memory-only credentials. Reloading that page discards them. Retired submission IDs remain in the deletion manifest and cannot recreate erased cases.
- Generic notification content must stay generic. Company administrators are responsible for policy/template wording and retention choices.
- Audit chains detect modification relative to retained checkpoints. A database/host owner can still replace local history; secure independent offsite checkpoint access is necessary.
- Backup snapshots containing held cases are kept until an authorized hold review. Deletion cannot remove expired personal data from an immutable held snapshot; restoration must replay the newest deletion and hold manifests before public access.
- Browser tests cover Chromium desktop and a mobile viewport, keyboard form controls, labels and layout. They do not replace a full assistive-technology or independent penetration test.

## Employee admission — 0.4.0

The privacy database owns encrypted employee records (name, email, international phone), email-verified access requests, admission challenges and opaque reporting grants. Normalized email lookup uses keyed hashes. Only the case API's MFA-protected administrator endpoints may call the signed directory endpoints; reviewers have no directory or grant-inspection API. Administrative changes produce durable, deduplicated audit events containing record IDs, never contacts or report references.

The homepage's **Create a report** link and direct `/report` visits both open the email gate before exposing manual or AI intake. Unknown and inactive emails are rejected without a report OTP. The gate offers retry/cancel and administrator contact guidance, with no self-registration or access-request form. Existing purpose-separated access-request APIs and administrator review remain separate from this reporting flow.

`/privacy/admission/challenge` distinguishes registered, unknown and disabled addresses as a deliberate product choice; membership lookup is rate limited. Report and request OTPs are purpose-separated, single-use, expire after five minutes and permit five guesses. Resends invalidate earlier challenges/proofs and queued expired/superseded codes are skipped. Destination budgets are one send per minute, five per hour and twenty per 24 hours. Request proofs expire after 15 minutes and may submit only the verified email; pending requests are deduplicated. Administrator approval creates an employee atomically, never overwrites/reactivates an existing employee, and queues a generic decision notice to the verified request address.

A reporting grant lasts two hours, permits one report and one AI conversation, and caps AI work at 30 message attempts and three draft-generation attempts. A nonblocking database transaction lock prevents concurrent AI operations on a grant. Failed upstream attempts still consume quota. Reverification using the same destination and old grant can resume an unfinished report without resetting counters. Deactivation or email changes revoke unfinished grants and challenges; they do not revoke access to previously submitted anonymous cases.

Report submission requires the existing retry credentials plus an `x-report-access` header. The signed privacy reservation binds the grant to one submission, claims its own intake if supplied, and clears its employee association atomically before case persistence. Reservations survive interrupted case commits and allow only the same submission for 24 hours. Admission tokens never enter case content or audit metadata. Already saved case PIN/recovery access and optional case-contact verification remain separate. Ordinary application roles cannot link employees to reports; privileged infrastructure operators and timing/content clues remain outside that boundary.

Expired challenges and abandoned grants are deleted after one day; anonymous retry records expire one day after reservation. Resolved access requests follow the company's contact retention period. Active directory entries remain until administratively managed. Private key rotation includes new encrypted fields, backups include the privacy tables, and restore revokes admission challenges/proofs/grants and removes queued admission email before the restored suite starts.

## Form-first reporting and private case follow-up — version 0.7.0

The current reporter journey is registered-email OTP, form, acknowledged credential receipt, then `/case`. The form includes optional why/how fields and optional MiniMax consent for processing the submitted report plus follow-up chat. No AI work occurs before the case is submitted and opened. This supersedes the pre-submission AI workflow described in historical validation sections above.

Reporter capabilities and signed session-validated context calls authorize the case-linked assistant. Raw conversations, context and generated review drafts remain encrypted in the privacy service. The interviewer uses existing form details to ask missing questions or confirm complete information, accepts unknowns, and does not claim investigative authority. Reporters edit and review a structured update before sharing it through the existing case mailbox; original form content is preserved.

Private conversation operations commit the reporter text, operation lease and UTC daily usage before calling the provider. At most one operation runs per conversation. Request IDs deduplicate replays; explicit retries refer to the failed operation and charge a new attempt without appending the reporter message again. Process interruptions become retryable after the two-minute lease. Case-message client IDs deduplicate approved publication, including its audit and notification. ## ISO 37002:2021 whistleblowing management alignment — version 1.0.0

Version 1.0.0 (initially introduced in 0.8.0) addresses the six ISO 37002:2021 (_Whistleblowing management systems — Guidelines_) gaps while preserving zero-contact-reveal and split-database isolation (`case-db` vs. `private-db`):

- **Clause 8.2 (Receiving reports of wrongdoing — broader stakeholder scope & relationship)**: Administrators can configure `admissionMode` in company settings (`directory`, `directory_or_request`, or `any_verified_email`), defaulting to `directory`. When `directory_or_request` is active, unregistered email challenges return `canRequestAccess: true` and the reporting gate offers a self-service OTP verification and access-request submission flow to `/privacy/admission/request/*`. When `any_verified_email` is active, any non-disabled email receives a reporting OTP without creating a directory record. The report form captures `reporterRelationship` (`employee`, `contractor_supplier`, `former_staff`, `shareholder_board`, `external_public`, or `prefer_not_to_say`) inside the encrypted case report.
- **Clauses 7.2, 7.3 & 8.4 (Leadership, resources, whistleblower protection & detriment management)**: Settings store an optional public `governanceRolesNote` describing WMF accountability and impartiality roles. Case triage and assessment record `detrimentRisk` (`none`, `low`, `medium`, `high`) and an encrypted `protectionPlan` (`protection_plan_enc` rotated by `cli/rekey.ts`). Reporters can submit dedicated retaliation/detriment reports via `POST /api/reporter/detriment` from `/case` even on closed cases, creating an encrypted `detriment` case record, setting `detriment_flagged = true`, auditing `reporter_detriment_reported`, and notifying assigned staff and team leads. Staff can clear the active detriment alert via `POST /api/cases/:id/detriment/resolve` after reviewing protection measures.
- **Clause 8.4.2 & 8.5 (Subject of report procedural fairness & right to be heard)**: Case closure proposals and approvals (`POST /api/cases/:id/closure`) accept a structured `proceduralFairness` attestation (`conflictChecked`, `subjectConfidentialityProtected`, `subjectResponseStatus` in `heard | not_contacted_prejudice | not_applicable`, and `subjectResponseRationale`), which is stored in the closure record and required when `subjectResponseStatus` is `not_contacted_prejudice`.
- **Clause 8.6 & 10.2 (Root-cause categorization & systemic vs. individual actions)**: Corrective actions (`case_actions`) capture optional `rootCause` (`control_gap`, `policy_clarity`, `training_awareness`, `management_oversight`, `individual_misconduct`, `system_process`, `other`) and `systemic` boolean flags.
- **Clause 9.1 & 9.2 (Post-closure anonymous reporter survey & effectiveness metrics)**: Closed cases allow a one-time anonymous reporter feedback submission via `POST /api/reporter/feedback` (`trustRating`, `communicationRating`, `protectionRating`, `processEaseRating` 1–5, plus optional `comment` without PII), stored in `cases.feedback` and audited as `reporter_feedback_submitted`. `GET /api/analytics` and `/staff/analytics` aggregate SLA compliance percentages (`ackSlaCompliancePct`, `dueSlaCompliancePct`), average cycle times (`avgTriageHours`, `avgResolutionHours`), detriment risk and flagged counts, root-cause breakdowns, systemic vs. individual action counts, reporter relationship distribution, and reporter survey averages alongside existing operational counters.
